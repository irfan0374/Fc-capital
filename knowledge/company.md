# About FC Capital

## What is FC Capital?
FC Capital is an online trading brand with the tagline "A clearer perspective". It gives clients access to global markets, including forex, indices and commodities, through the MetaTrader 5 platform on desktop and mobile. New clients apply online through a guided application, and the FC Capital team reviews every application before account access.

## Which markets can I trade with FC Capital?
FC Capital presents three market groups:
- Forex: currency pairs such as EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY.
- Indices: index CFDs such as the US 500, US Tech and UK 100.
- Commodities: metal and energy CFDs such as gold, silver and crude oil.
Product availability depends on the client's country of residence. Full contract specifications, trading hours and costs are to be confirmed before launch.

## Which trading platform does FC Capital use?
FC Capital uses MetaTrader 5 (MT5), available on desktop and mobile. MT5 brings charts, market analysis and the trading workspace together. Download links, account access details and supported devices are to be confirmed before launch.

## What account types does FC Capital offer?
FC Capital proposes two account types:
- Standard account: "a simple start", with a spread-based pricing structure (costs built into the spread).
- Raw account: "a different approach", with a commission-based pricing structure (tighter spreads plus a separate commission).
Both use MetaTrader 5. Spreads, commissions and the minimum deposit are to be confirmed. Leverage is subject to eligibility.

## Risk warning
CFDs are complex instruments and carry a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. You should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money. Nothing FC Capital publishes is an offer to trade or investment advice.
