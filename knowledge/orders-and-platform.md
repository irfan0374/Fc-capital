# Orders, charts and MetaTrader 5

## What is a market order?
A market order buys or sells immediately at the best available price. It prioritises speed of execution over an exact price, so the fill price can differ slightly from the quote in fast markets.

## What is a limit order?
A limit order is placed to buy or sell at a specific price or better. A buy limit is placed below the current price, and a sell limit above it. It only fills if the market reaches that price.

## What is a stop order?
A stop order becomes a market order once a set price is reached. A buy stop is placed above the current price, and a sell stop below it. Traders use stop orders to enter on a breakout, or to exit a losing trade (stop-loss).

## What is a candlestick chart?
A candlestick shows the open, high, low and close prices for one time period (for example 1 hour). The body shows the open-to-close range; the thin lines (wicks) show the high and low. A rising candle closes above its open; a falling candle closes below it.

## What are timeframes?
A timeframe is the period each candle or bar represents, such as 1 minute (M1), 1 hour (H1) or 1 day (D1). Short timeframes show more detail and noise; longer timeframes show the bigger trend.

## What are support and resistance?
Support is a price area where falling prices have tended to stop and bounce. Resistance is an area where rising prices have tended to stop. They are not guaranteed; prices can break through them.

## What are technical indicators?
Indicators are calculations shown on a chart to help analyse price. Common examples are moving averages (trend), RSI (momentum, overbought or oversold) and MACD (trend and momentum). No indicator predicts the future with certainty.

## What is fundamental analysis versus technical analysis?
Fundamental analysis studies economic data, interest rates, company results and news to judge value and direction. Technical analysis studies price charts, patterns and indicators. Many traders combine both.

## What is MetaTrader 5?
MetaTrader 5 (MT5) is a widely used trading platform for desktop and mobile. It offers real-time charts, many timeframes, built-in indicators, different order types, one-click trading and automated trading with Expert Advisors. FC Capital uses MT5 for both the Standard and Raw accounts.

## How do I place a trade in MetaTrader 5?
In MT5, open a chart of the instrument, click "New Order" (or the one-click buy/sell buttons), choose the volume (lot size), add a stop-loss and take-profit if you want, and choose Buy or Sell. Open trades appear in the Trade tab of the Toolbox, where you can modify or close them.
