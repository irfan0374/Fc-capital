# Opening an account with FC Capital

## How do I open an account?
Click "Open an account" or "Start your journey" on the FC Capital website. The online application has eight steps:
1. Contact details: name, email, phone and country of residence.
2. Email verification: enter the code sent to your email.
3. Personal information: date of birth, nationality, address and tax details.
4. Financial profile: employment, income, net worth, expected deposit and source of funds.
5. Trading experience: your experience, how often you trade and a short knowledge check.
6. Account preferences: account type (Standard or Raw) and other preferences.
7. Documents: identity verification with your camera and a proof of address.
8. Review and submit: check everything, then submit. You receive a reference number such as FC-1A2B3C4D.

## Can I apply from my phone or my laptop?
Yes. You can apply from a phone or a laptop. The identity step needs a camera. On a laptop, a QR code appears: scan it with your phone, take the photos on the phone, and the laptop page updates automatically. Keep the laptop page open while you do this.

## Which identity documents do I need?
It depends on your country of residence:
- United Arab Emirates residents: Emirates ID, passport or driving licence.
- Other countries: passport, national ID card or driving licence.
For an ID card or driving licence you photograph the front and the back. For a passport you photograph the photo page only.
You also need a selfie and a proof of address.

## How does the ID photo work?
Place your document inside the frame on screen. The camera captures it automatically as soon as the card is steady, sharp and fully inside the frame. You don't need to press a button. You can retake a photo before using it. Tips: use good light, put the card on a plain dark surface, and avoid glare and blur.

## How does the selfie or face check work?
The selfie includes a quick liveness check to make sure a real person is present. Look at the screen, blink once, then look straight at the camera. The photo is taken automatically.

## What counts as proof of address?
A recent document that shows your full name and current residential address, for example a utility bill, bank statement or official letter. Upload it as a photo (JPEG/PNG) or PDF, up to 4 MB.

## Are my documents safe?
Yes. Your documents are stored privately and are only visible to the FC Capital review team. The secure camera link expires after your application is submitted.

## What happens after I submit my application?
Your application goes to the FC Capital team for review. The team checks your details and documents and then approves the application, asks for more information, or declines it. They will contact you with the next steps. Submitting an application does not by itself create a trading account.

## How long does the review take?
Review times are not yet published. The FC Capital team will contact you after reviewing your application.

## What if my document is not accepted?
If something is unclear, such as a blurry photo or an expired document, the FC Capital team will request more information and explain what is needed.
