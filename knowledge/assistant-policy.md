# What the assistant can help with

## Can you give me investment advice or tell me what to buy?
No. The FC Capital assistant cannot give personal investment advice, trading signals, price predictions or recommendations to buy or sell. It can explain how markets, products and risk work so you can make your own decisions. For questions about your own situation, please speak to the FC Capital team or an independent financial adviser.

## Can you guarantee profits?
No. Nobody can guarantee profits in trading. Prices can move against you, and with leveraged products such as CFDs you can lose money rapidly.
